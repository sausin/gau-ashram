## Join Our Mission of Transformation

Volunteering at Gau Mandir is more than service—it's a transformative journey that connects you with nature, traditional wisdom, and purposeful living. Whether you can spare a weekend or several months, your contribution makes a real difference.

### Volunteer Programs

#### 1. Goshala Seva (Cow Service)

**Daily Activities:**
- Morning feeding and care routines
- Cleaning and maintaining shelters
- Preparing natural fodder
- Collecting cow products for medicines
- Bonding with gentle beings

**What You'll Learn:**
- Animal communication and behavior
- Traditional cow care practices
- Preparation of Panchgavya products
- Spiritual significance of cow service

**Duration:** Minimum 1 week

#### 2. Natural Healing Assistant

**Responsibilities:**
- Assist in patient consultations
- Help prepare natural medicines
- Support therapy sessions
- Document treatment outcomes
- Maintain healing spaces

**What You'll Learn:**
- Basics of naturopathy and Panchgavya
- Patient care and empathy
- Medicine preparation techniques
- Traditional healing protocols

**Duration:** Minimum 2 weeks

#### 3. Sustainable Farming Helper

**Activities:**
- Seed sowing and transplanting
- Preparing natural fertilizers
- Mulching and composting
- Harvesting and processing
- Water conservation work

**What You'll Learn:**
- Chemical-free farming methods
- Soil health management
- Traditional seed preservation
- Farm-to-table processes

**Duration:** Minimum 1 month (full crop cycle preferred)

#### 4. Education and Outreach

**Responsibilities:**
- Teach in rural schools
- Conduct health awareness camps
- Document traditional knowledge
- Create educational materials
- Social media management

**What You'll Learn:**
- Community engagement skills
- Traditional knowledge systems
- Rural development challenges
- Communication and teaching

**Duration:** Minimum 3 weeks

#### 5. Construction and Maintenance

**Projects:**
- Building eco-friendly structures
- Creating water harvesting systems
- Maintaining gardens and landscapes
- Solar installation support
- Traditional architecture preservation

**What You'll Learn:**
- Sustainable construction techniques
- Water conservation methods
- Natural building materials
- Community development

**Duration:** Project-based

### Life as a Volunteer

#### Daily Schedule
- **5:00 AM**: Wake up and personal time
- **6:00 AM**: Yoga and meditation
- **7:00 AM**: Breakfast and cow service
- **8:30 AM**: Assigned work begins
- **1:00 PM**: Lunch and rest
- **3:00 PM**: Afternoon activities
- **6:00 PM**: Evening prayers/satsang
- **7:00 PM**: Dinner
- **8:00 PM**: Study time/cultural programs
- **9:30 PM**: Rest

#### What We Provide
- Simple, clean accommodation
- Nutritious vegetarian meals
- Work clothes and basic supplies
- Training and guidance
- Certificate of service
- Lifetime memories and learning

#### What to Bring
- Personal toiletries
- Comfortable work clothes
- Notebook and pen
- Open mind and heart
- Enthusiasm to learn
- Basic medicines if needed

### Application Process

1. **Online Application**: Fill out our detailed form
2. **Phone Interview**: Discuss your interests and availability
3. **Reference Check**: Provide two character references
4. **Confirmation**: Receive joining instructions
5. **Orientation**: On-arrival training and integration
