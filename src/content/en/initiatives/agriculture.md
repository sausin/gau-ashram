## Poison-Free Farming for Healthy Living

Our sustainable agriculture initiative promotes farming methods that heal the earth while producing nutritious food. We demonstrate that chemical-free farming is not only possible but more profitable and sustainable.

### Core Practices

#### Cow-Based Inputs
- **Jeevamrut**: Liquid microbial culture for soil health
- **Ghanjeevamrut**: Solid organic fertilizer
- **Beejamrut**: Seed treatment solution
- **Agniastra & Brahmastra**: Natural pest repellents

#### Soil Regeneration
- Mulching with crop residues
- Mixed cropping systems
- Traditional seed preservation
- Water conservation techniques

### Training Components

1. **Soil Health Assessment**
   - Understanding soil biology
   - Testing without chemicals
   - Identifying deficiencies naturally

2. **Input Preparation**
   - On-farm production of all inputs
   - Cost: Less than ₹500 per acre per year
   - Using local resources only

3. **Crop Management**
   - Companion planting strategies
   - Natural pest management
   - Harvest and post-harvest techniques

### Results Achieved

- **40% increase** in crop yield after 2 years
- **60% reduction** in water usage
- **100% elimination** of chemical costs
- **Premium prices** for organic produce
- **Improved soil carbon** content
- **Return of beneficial insects** and birds
