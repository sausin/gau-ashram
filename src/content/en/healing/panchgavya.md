## Holistic Healing from Sacred Cows

Panchgavya Chikitsa is an ancient Ayurvedic practice that uses five products from indigenous cows—milk, curd, ghee, urine, and dung—for treating various ailments. Modern research validates what our ancestors knew: these products have remarkable therapeutic properties.

### The Five Sacred Elements

#### 1. Milk (Dugdh)
- Rich in immunoglobulins and growth factors
- Promotes tissue regeneration
- Calms the mind and nourishes the body
- Used in medicated milk preparations

#### 2. Curd (Dadhi)
- Probiotic powerhouse for gut health
- Improves digestion and immunity
- Cooling effect on the body
- Used in skin treatments

#### 3. Ghee (Ghrita)
- Carrier for herbal medicines
- Enhances memory and intelligence
- Lubricates joints and tissues
- Anti-inflammatory properties

#### 4. Cow Urine (Gomutra)
- Natural detoxifier and germicide
- Contains 24 types of minerals
- Anticancer and antioxidant properties
- Used in various formulations

#### 5. Cow Dung (Gomaya)
- Antiseptic and antimicrobial
- Used in external applications
- Purifies environment
- Source of beneficial bacteria
