# Infrastructure Development Projects

To transform our gaushala into a model centre for cow-based rural development, we are working on the following projects:

## Current Projects

### 🏠 Construction of Modern Cow Shelters
- Ventilated and comfortable shelters
- Weather-resistant design
- Capacity for 100+ cows per shelter

### 🌱 Grazing Land Restoration
- Preservation of natural pasture
- Soil regeneration programs
- Native grass cultivation

### 💧 Water Infrastructure
- Installation of drinking water tanks
- Irrigation system development
- Rainwater harvesting structures

### ♻️ Sustainable Energy Projects
- Biogas plants for renewable energy
- Organic manure production units
- Waste management systems

### 🏭 Panchgavya Enterprise Units
- Micro-enterprise development
- Product processing facilities
- Quality control laboratories

### 🏥 Cow Healthcare Center (Gau Arogya Kendra)
- Veterinary clinic facilities
- Medical equipment and supplies
- Emergency care units
