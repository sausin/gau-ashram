## Transforming Lives and Land

Our work at Gau Mandir has created ripples of positive change across communities:

### Land Revival
**100+ acres** of degraded land transformed into thriving ecosystems through natural farming and water conservation.

### Water Conservation
Water table raised from **410 feet to 120 feet** through traditional harvesting methods, bringing life back to dry regions.

### Cattle Conservation
**197+ Tharparkar bulls** distributed to farmers, preserving indigenous breeds and supporting sustainable agriculture.

### Women Empowerment
**1000+ rural women** trained in natural healing, herbal medicine, and sustainable livelihood practices.

### Community Health
Thousands of families now have access to chemical-free healthcare through Panchgavya and naturopathy treatments.

### Economic Impact
Created sustainable income sources for hundreds of families through cow-based enterprises and organic farming.
