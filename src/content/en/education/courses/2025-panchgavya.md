# Panchgavya One-Year Course

## Course Nature

* Therapeutic and Practical

## Duration

* 12 months (2 semesters × 6 months)

## Objectives

* Understanding Panchgavya therapy principles
* Panchgavya medicine preparation and application
* Use of Panchgavya therapy in common and complex diseases
* Practice of Panchgavya-based Panchkarma

---

## 📚 **Semester I**

### Unit 1: Introduction to Panchgavya Therapy

1. Historical background of Panchgavya therapy
2. Principles and philosophical aspects
3. Therapeutic importance of the five elements of Panchgavya (milk, curd, ghee, urine, dung)
4. Importance of Upgavya (buttermilk, cow dung ash, extracts)

### Unit 2: Basic Knowledge of Body, Health and Disease

1. Human anatomy - basic introduction
2. Physiology - digestion, respiration, excretion, etc.
3. General definition of health and disease
4. Causes, symptoms and primary classification of diseases

### Unit 3: Basic Methods of Panchgavya Medicine Preparation

1. Cow urine extract, ghee, oil, tablets, paste - preparation process
2. Use of Panchgavya ghee and extract oils
3. Purification, storage and standards
4. Medicine preparation with drop-by-drop mixing

### Unit 4: Panchgavya Therapy in Common Diseases

1. Fever, cold, cough, acidity
2. Indigestion, constipation, hemorrhoids
3. Skin diseases, allergies, boils
4. Physical weakness and fatigue with Panchgavya

### Unit 5: Panchgavya Therapy in Chronic Diseases

1. Diabetes, high blood pressure
2. Arthritis, joint pain, osteoarthritis
3. Respiratory system related diseases and treatment
4. Supportive therapy in cancer, thyroid, etc.

### Unit 6: Panchgavya Therapy in Women's and Men's Diseases

1. Infertility, menstrual disorders, uterine diseases
2. Prostate, semen disorders, sexual weakness
3. Use in infant and child diseases

---

## 📚 **Semester II**

### Unit 7: Panchkarma and Special Treatment Methods with Panchgavya

1. Vamana, Virechana, Basti
2. Nasya, Shirodhara, Abhyanga, Swedana
3. Cow urine Basti, cow ghee Purana therapy
4. Panchgavya bath, paste, consumption

### Unit 8: Immunity, Daily Routine and Yogic Lifestyle

1. Panchgavya daily routine: morning to night usage
2. Panchgavya-yoga combination for disease prevention
3. Importance of Panchgavya in seasonal and dietary routines
4. Role of Panchgavya in mental health

### Unit 9: Cow Health Management

1. Importance of cow health management
2. Nutrition and diet - green fodder, dry fodder, oil cake, salt, clean water
3. Housing and cleanliness - ventilated cowshed, regular cleaning, pest control
4. Inspection and regular examination
5. Common diseases and Panchgavya-based treatment
6. Reproductive health - causes of infertility, prevention and care
7. Emergency situations and first aid
8. Health recording and monitoring

### Unit 10: Gaushala Management

1. Purpose and importance
   * Contribution from religious, social, economic and therapeutic perspectives

2. Site selection and construction planning
   * Drainage, air and sunlight flow, shed height, arrangements for newborn and elderly cows

3. Classification and care of cows
   * Cleaning, bathing, brushing, disinfectant spraying for pregnant, milking, calf, bull, etc.

4. Food and water management
   * Nutritional balance, green and dry fodder, water availability

5. Staff management and job descriptions
   * Number of servants, responsibilities, ethical conduct and training

6. Product manufacturing and use
   * Product manufacturing and use from dung, urine, milk, curd
   * Cow products for medical and agricultural purposes

7. Accounting and record management
   * Number of cattle, income-expenditure, details of donors

8. Model Gaushala plan for overall development
   * Self-reliance, organic farming, training center, tourism
