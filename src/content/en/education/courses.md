## Academic Excellence Meets Ancient Knowledge

In partnership with **Dr. Sarvapalli Radhakrishnan Rajasthan Ayurved University**, we offer certified courses that blend traditional wisdom with academic rigor. Our programs prepare you for a career in natural healing while preserving India's heritage.

### Certified Diploma Programs

#### 1. Diploma in Panchgavya Chikitsa (6 Months)

**Course Content:**
- Fundamentals of Panchgavya science
- Preparation methods and quality control
- Clinical applications and protocols
- Research methodology
- Practical training with patients

**Career Opportunities:**
- Panchgavya therapy practitioner
- Wellness consultant
- Product development specialist
- Research assistant

**Eligibility:** 12th pass or equivalent

#### 2. Certificate in Naturopathy & Sustainable Living (3 Months)

**Course Content:**
- Five element therapy principles
- Diet and lifestyle management
- Yoga and meditation basics
- Sustainable farming introduction
- Community health practices

**Career Opportunities:**
- Naturopathy assistant
- Wellness coach
- Community health worker
- Organic farming advisor

**Eligibility:** 10th pass or equivalent

#### 3. Rural Entrepreneurship Through Cow-Based Economy (3 Months)

**Course Content:**
- Goshala management
- Product manufacturing (soaps, medicines)
- Marketing and branding
- Financial planning
- Cooperative formation

**Career Opportunities:**
- Entrepreneur in cow-based products
- Goshala manager
- Cooperative society member
- Rural development facilitator

**Eligibility:** Basic literacy

### Learning Methodology

Our unique approach includes:

1. **Gurukul System**: Living and learning in nature
2. **Practical Training**: 70% hands-on experience
3. **Traditional Texts**: Study of authentic scriptures
4. **Modern Science**: Understanding through research
5. **Community Practice**: Real-world application

### Certification Benefits

- **University Recognition**: Certificates from renowned Ayurved University
- **Government Jobs**: Eligibility for various positions
