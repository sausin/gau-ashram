## Ancient Wisdom for Modern Wellness

Naturopathy recognizes that the human body has an inherent ability to heal itself when given the right conditions. Our treatments use the five elements—earth, water, fire, air, and ether—to restore balance and promote natural healing.

### Treatment Modalities

#### Mud Therapy (Earth Element)
- **Mud packs** for inflammation and pain relief
- **Mud baths** for skin conditions and detoxification
- **Clay applications** for wound healing
- Temperature-specific treatments for various conditions

#### Hydrotherapy (Water Element)
- Hip baths for digestive and reproductive health
- Steam therapy for respiratory conditions
- Hot and cold compresses for pain management
- Enemas for detoxification

#### Sun Therapy (Fire Element)
- Chromotherapy using colored glass
- Morning sun exposure for Vitamin D
- Specific timing for different conditions
- Integration with yoga practices

#### Air Therapy (Air Element)
- Pranayama (breathing exercises)
- Fresh air exposure protocols
- Oxygen-rich environment therapy
- Walking meditation in nature

#### Fasting Therapy (Ether Element)
- Intermittent fasting protocols
- Juice fasting for detoxification
- Water fasting under supervision
- Breaking fast with proper diet

### Conditions Treated

Our naturopathy center successfully treats:
- Chronic digestive disorders
- Respiratory conditions
- Skin diseases
- Joint and muscle pain
- Stress and anxiety
- Metabolic disorders
- Autoimmune conditions
