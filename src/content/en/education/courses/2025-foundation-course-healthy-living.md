## 1. **Preamble**

This foundation course introduces **Self-Reliant Healing Science** to promote sustainable, healthy lifestyles in modern society. It offers knowledge of self-care techniques using traditional, non-invasive methods. Participants will learn the names, processes, usage methods, and practical applications of these healing modalities to:

* Maintain personal health
* Prevent disease
* Manage chronic lifestyle disorders

## 11. **Course Content (Unit-wise)**

---

### 📘 **Unit 1: Introduction to Body and Health**

1. Human anatomy and basic functions
2. Common causes of disease
3. Daily routine and habits
4. Seasonal routines
5. Role of food, lifestyle, and emotions
6. Self-reliant healing approaches

---

### 📘 **Unit 2: Panchmahabhuta (Five Elements) & Natural Science**

* Concepts of **Space, Air, Fire, Water, Earth** and their role in human health
* Role in creating balance and disease
* Elemental therapies and corrections

---

### 📘 **Unit 3: Sunlight Therapy**

* Sun gazing techniques
* Water, oil, and medicine preparation using sunlight
* Benefits and precautions

---

### 📘 **Unit 4: Color Therapy**

* Psychological and physiological impact of colors
* Identifying health issues via color preferences
* Color therapy techniques including water, cloth, and environmental use

---

### 📘 **Unit 5: Water and Oil Therapies**

* Proper water-drinking techniques and timing
* Methods of oil and salt massage
* Water-based and oil-based detox therapies
* Enema therapy

---

### 📘 **Unit 6: Cow-Based Therapies**

* Cow dung, urine, curd, ghee, etc. for treatment
* Formulation and application methods
* Internal and external therapies

---

### 📘 **Unit 7: Touch and Pressure Therapies**

* Pressure-point science
* Diagnosing via pain & imbalance
* Pressure application, pain relief techniques
* Manual therapy and proper massage

---

### 📘 **Unit 8: Herbal and Kitchen Remedies**

* Tulsi, Haldi, Ginger, Neem, Ashwagandha, Mulethi, etc.
* Usage in acute and chronic diseases
* Home-prepared formulations
