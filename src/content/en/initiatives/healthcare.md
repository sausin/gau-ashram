## Empowering Communities with Natural Healing

We believe healthcare should be accessible, affordable, and rooted in nature. Our training programs equip rural families with the knowledge and skills to become health champions in their communities.

### Training Programs

#### Acupressure Therapy
- Diagnosis through pressure points
- Treatment of common ailments
- Preventive health practices
- No equipment or medicine needed

#### Naturopathy Basics
- Five element therapy
- Mud therapy applications
- Hydrotherapy techniques
- Diet and lifestyle counseling

#### Panchgavya Chikitsa
- Preparation of cow-based medicines
- Treatment protocols
- Quality control methods
- Integration with modern healthcare

### Impact on Communities

- **5000+ practitioners** trained across villages
- **80% reduction** in healthcare expenses for trained families
- **Zero side effects** reported in treatments
- **Revival of traditional knowledge** preserved for future generations

### Training Methodology

Our approach combines:
- Hands-on practical sessions
- Traditional knowledge documentation
- Case study discussions
- Follow-up support and mentoring
- Community health camps for practice
