# Hindu Calendar-Based Special Donation Days

In Indian tradition, cow service and cow donations (Gau Daan) are especially meritorious on select holy days. We organize special services on these sacred occasions:

## Sacred Calendar for Gau Seva

| Date | Occasion | Ideal Donation |
|------|----------|----------------|
| **Gopashtami** | Cow worship festival | Panchgavya bath, fodder donation |
| **Govatsa Dwadashi** | For child blessings | Gau Aarti, jaggery sugar donation |
| **Purnima / Amavasya** | In memory of ancestors | Gau Daan in Pitru's name |
| **Ekadashi / Sankranti** | For inner purification | Cow feeding, water donation |
| **Holi / Diwali** | For good fortune | Support cow-care kits, incense units |
