# Our Story

Lakshya Paryawaran and Jankalyan Sanstha is a grassroots organization dedicated to ecological restoration, indigenous cow conservation, natural healing, and rural empowerment. Founded to revive Bharatiya parampara through self-reliant practices, we blend traditional knowledge with sustainable innovation to serve communities and protect nature.

## The Beginning

Our journey began with a simple observation: the disconnect between modern life and our traditional wisdom was causing harm to both people and the environment. We saw degraded lands, vanishing indigenous cattle breeds, and communities losing touch with natural healing practices that had sustained them for generations.

## Our Approach

We believe in the power of indigenous knowledge systems, particularly the role of native cattle breeds in maintaining ecological balance. Our work centers around the **Go Mandir** concept - not just as a shelter for cows, but as a hub for:

- **Ecological restoration** through natural farming methods
- **Community empowerment** through skill development
- **Health and wellness** through traditional healing practices
- **Economic sustainability** through cow-based enterprises

## The Impact

What started as a small initiative has grown into a movement that has:

- Restored over 1000 acres of degraded land
- Raised water tables from 410 feet to 120 feet
- Trained thousands of rural women in natural healing
- Distributed 197 Tharparkar bulls to farmers
- Created sustainable livelihoods for countless families

Our work continues to expand, touching more lives and healing more land with each passing day.
